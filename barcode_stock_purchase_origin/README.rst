This module exposes the purchase order origin on incoming receipts and
in the Barcode picking list.

It adds a read-only ``purchase_origin`` field on ``stock.picking``,
related to the purchase order origin. The value is shown on the incoming
Receipt transfer view, below the transfer name in the Barcode picking
list for incoming transfers, and is included in the Barcode picking
search.
