Nothing to configure: install the module and the picking screen follows the
work.

- Complete one move (its wizard's **Confirm move**, or a scan that fills it)
  while other moves are pending: the screen stays on **To Do**.
- Complete the last move of the picking: the screen lands on **Done**.
- Remove a completed line from the **Done** tab: the screen stays there, even
  if other moves are still pending.
- Coming back from another screen (the responsible picker, for instance)
  restores the tab you left open.
